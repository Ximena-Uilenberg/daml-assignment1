# daml-assignment1

## The dataset
The dataset contains a selection of metadata from different pieces from the Euromeana website. Most of the selected pieces are of Greek or Roman goddesses. All the pieces are from the 369 CARARE UNIBO collection provided by the University of Bologna in Italy. There are 20 items in total, each a different piece from the collection, such as a statue or a relief. 

## Motivation
I created this dataset because I wanted to understand how the data 'processing' worked with museum items since this might become useful for my thesis. The type of research questions that can be answered from this small dataset would be around the topics of marble work in the 1st to 2nd centuries BC coming from Italy or specifically the city of Bologna. It could be centered around sculpting methods or the sculpting subjects most depicted in marble statues. 

## Selection criteria
I hand picked the items for the dataset based on the intricate pictures of the marble statues but I decided not to add the pictures to the data.csv because I did not yet understand how to do that. Overall, I picked this selection because I liked the variety of Greek goddesses sculpted in marble compared to the male statues. My dataset is very limited and only represents less than a fraction of the collection I selected them from. It is missing a lot of marble reliefs, male statues of kings and gods, reliefs for vaults, boxes or chests made out marble or metal during the same time period. 

## Data collection process
I collected the items from the [Europeana website](https://www.europeana.eu/en/collections/topic/26-archaeological-artefact?page=1&qf=TYPE%3A%22IMAGE%22&qf=LANGUAGE%3A%22en%22&qf=COUNTRY%3A%22Italy%22&qf=DATA_PROVIDER%3A%22University%20of%20Bologna%22&reusability=open) under the University of Bologna collections which allowed the use of the online repository under the licence [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). I copied the information one at a time from the 'metadata' tab under the image and description, and pasted it into an Excel file which I later saved as an .csv file. The information was collected on the 17th of Novermber 2026.

## Uses and limitations
The data set can be used as an example of what the 369 CARARE UNIBO collection at Europeana can offer but it cannot give a full picture of all the items in the collection. It can be used for a limited research essay.
